    Controls

    Free camera: W A S D to move, Q and E for down and up, Shift to go faster, drag to look, scroll to change speed.
    Locked camera: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Selecting a planet frames it at local scale; scroll out for a system-wide view. Click a body or use the list to lock on, B locks the black hole, X goes back to the free camera.
    Space pauses, comma and period change the speed. T toggles trails, L labels, H hides the interface, P takes a screenshot. Send toward hole pushes the locked body and its nearby system toward the hole. Lock a planet, then use the Planet Surface button; on a solid world, WASD walks and drag looks around. On gas giants, WASD flies through cloud layers, Q/E changes altitude, and Esc returns to orbit. The simulation models clouds, terrain, spin/day-night, tilt and seasonal lighting procedurally; orbit periods are reference values while positions evolve under the game's gravity model.
